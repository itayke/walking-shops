# Milestones

Each milestone ends with something you can run and look at.

1. **Simulation core.** ✓ Done: tick-for-tick parity with the prototype, bench within noise. Port `reference/sim.js` to TypeScript. Rules, waves (with the bar telegraph), parachutes, idle shutters, scoring, bots. Unit tests per movement rule, invariant checks, golden replays. A headless bench script that matches the prototype's numbers within noise.
2. **Screen renderer, offline.** ✓ Done. Street, shops with legs, shutters, crosswalk bars, shoppers, parachuters, strain wobble, topple arcs. Keyboard plays one shop; bots play the rest. Debug panel with every tunable. Restyled in pixel art (see "Look" in `GAME_DESIGN.md`).
3. **Networking.** Local build done: relay, phone controller, reconnect, 50-phone load test (p95 input-to-ack ≈ 140 ms on a local relay). Still to do: deploy to Cloudflare, load test the deployed room, and a playtest with real phones on cellular. PartyServer room on Cloudflare. Phone controller (two halves: tap to step, hold to walk; send presses as events so a quick tap is never lost between ticks). Reconnect tokens. Fake-phone load test with 50 clients. Bot phones that join a real screen's room, for rehearsals with a few real phones.
4. **Join flow.** ✓ Done. A lobby: title and QR over a brawl without consequences, shops dropping in as players open them. Phone form with a live preview (shop name on the sign as you type, swipe through shop types, assigned color). Presenter list to rename or remove shops, a begin key that fills with bots up to `minPlayers`, and a replay that reopens the lobby.
5. **End card.** Done on the `podium-end-card` branch: the talk ends on the game (the town hub was dropped). The sim takes the controls, walks everyone aside, drops the podium's blocks and walks the winners up, then hands play back until the next round. Ranking and contact card on screen, the player's result and the full contact (vCard, links) on the phone. Still to do: fill in the contact details in `copy.ts`.
6. **Stage readiness.** Presenter controls done: begin, skip (`S`), restart (`N`), and the bot fallback (`F`, and on its own when the relay drops). Still to do: projector readability check at 30 shops, rehearsal with real phones.
