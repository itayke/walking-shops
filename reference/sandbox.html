Rules in this build

  • Walk or sit. Hold a direction to walk, release to sit. Release before the half-cell mark and you spring back.
  • Walking stands one floor taller. Legs grow from the underside of the store (height is a slider, default one full floor). Your legs reach one floor, so you climb a store one level above your feet.
  • One rule for neighbors. Lower: step down. Level: walk across the roof. One up: climb. Two or more up: push.
  • Push to topple. Hold against a tower for the push time and everything above your head falls to the far column, landing on top of whatever is there. Then you climb in.
  • Push back. The store you're pushing can walk into you; that bumps you both and resets the push.
  • Toppled stores are stunned briefly and can't be pushed again for the immunity time.
  • Head-on walkers bump. Two walkers pressing toward each other within one floor bounce apart.
  • Carrying. A walking store carries everything above it. Carried stores still count as sitting.
  • Walk-ins cross the street toward the shop in front of them and enter the lowest open, sitting shop in that column. Walking shops get skipped; shoppers pass under the legs. With nowhere to go (within the sidestep range), they walk back in grey.
  • Crosswalk waves. A crosswalk a few columns wide appears (dashed, with a countdown), a wave of shoppers crosses it, then it fades and reappears somewhere else after a pause. A thin trickle of walk-ins arrives anywhere, all the time.
  • Idle shutters. A shop with no input for the idle time pulls its shutters down; the shutters drop visibly during the warning. Closed shops earn nothing: walk-ins go upstairs past them, parachuters pass them by. Any press reopens. Climb onto a closed shop and its customers come up to you.
  • Other arrival modes from earlier builds are still in the menu: a drifting crowd band, and fixed crosswalks where shoppers walk to the nearest sitting shop anywhere.
  • Parachuters fall in phase two, drift with the wind, and enter the first store they touch, walking or not. Parachuters see walking shops at their full height. Ones that reach the street become walk-ins.
  • Scoring. A parachuter can be worth more than a walk-in, and phase 1 earnings can count for less. When a tower catches a parachuter, the payout goes to the top shop only, is split between floors, or pays every floor the full value. Walk-ins can optionally pay the tower the same way.
  • Decided for this build: edge towers can't be toppled (nowhere to fall). Bots yield sometimes after a head-on bump.
